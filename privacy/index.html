Privacy Policy

This policy explains how EOS Villas looks after your personal information. Last updated August 2026.

1. Who we are

EOS Villas is a specialist luxury villa holiday company. We are the data controller responsible for the personal information we collect and process through this website.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number and booking details when you make an enquiry or booking.

We also collect limited technical information automatically, such as your IP address and how you use our site, to help us improve your experience.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, process bookings, arrange your stay, provide customer support, and — where you have agreed — keep you informed of relevant offers and destination news.

4. Legal basis for processing

We rely on the performance of a contract to process bookings, on legitimate interest for customer service and marketing, and on your consent where required by law.

5. Sharing your information

We share the minimum information necessary with villa owners and local support teams to arrange your stay. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Data retention

We keep your information only for as long as is necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us.

8. Contact

To make a privacy request or ask a question about this policy, please email info@eosvillas.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.
