Expert Tips for Costa del Sol

Andalusian Heritage

  • •Visit the Balcon de Europa at sunset for the best views.
  • •Try the local 'Miel de Caña' (Cane Honey) from El Ingenio.
  • •Cueva de Nerja — 42,000-year-old cave paintings and some of the world's largest stalactites — the Gallery of Cataclysms chamber is genuinely jaw-dropping.
  • •Chiringuito Ayo — Legendary wood-fired paella cooked in giant pans on the sand since 1969 — winner of Spain's "Foods of Spain" award. Paella still around €7 a plate.
  • •Cala del Cañuelo — Most remote and pristine cove near Nerja, in the Natural Park — crystal-clear water ideal for snorkelling over orange coral. Bring water shoes, arrive before noon.
