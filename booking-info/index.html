How It Works

Booking with EOS Villas

A simple, transparent booking experience designed around you. Here is exactly how it works from enquiry to arrival.

The Booking Journey

Step 1

Choose your villa

Browse our handpicked collection and shortlist the villas you love. Not sure? Use Smart Match and our team will suggest the perfect fit.

Step 2

Secure with 10% deposit

A refundable 10% deposit confirms your booking. The balance is due 8 weeks before arrival.

Step 3

Meet & greet on arrival

Our local representative meets you at the villa, hands over the keys and shows you around.

Step 4

24/7 local support

Throughout your stay our team is a phone call away, ready to help or arrange extras.

Flexible booking

We understand plans change. Our 10% refundable deposit gives you genuine flexibility on dates and destinations.

ATOL & financial protection

Book with total confidence knowing your money is fully protected under our financial protection arrangements.
